Pictures and credits

  • Barramundi fish (the silver fish in the reef schools): a real 3D model, "BarramundiFish", by Microsoft, shared under CC0 (public domain). Found in the Khronos glTF-Sample-Models collection: github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Models. I shrank its textures to save space.
  • Everything else (water, light, coral, sharks, turtle, manta, whales, jellyfish, squid, anglerfish, vents, wreck) is built by code. These are 3D models made from simple shapes, so they are stylised, not photographs, and they are not to scale or exact in colour.
  • The sound is made live in your browser. There are no recordings.
